title: "Unveiling the composition and origins of inhalable submicron particles from electric vehicle thermal runaway"

Particles collected during a full-scale electric-vehicle thermal-runaway experiment were examined using single-particle mass spectrometry and several classification methods. The analysis distinguished electrolyte-derived organic particles, electrode decomposition products, and particles formed through interactions with the surrounding atmosphere. Electrolyte-related material dominated particle numbers, while electrode-related particles carried potentially harmful metal and fluorine-containing species. Respiratory deposition calculations indicated substantial delivery to the alveolar and upper-airway regions, providing evidence for source-specific exposure assessment and improved battery-fire response.
