A two-stage learning framework addresses the need for rapid, detailed predictions of accidental atmospheric releases. First, a CNN–GRU model learns dispersion behavior from GRAMM/GRAL simulations spanning different release positions, wind conditions, and building arrangements. A subsequent assimilation stage incorporates point measurements and information about sources and nearby structures. Graph neural networks represent spatial relationships among observations so that measurements can refine predicted concentration fields. The approach aims to combine computational speed with adaptable steady-state dispersion estimates in complex urban settings.
