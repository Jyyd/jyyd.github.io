Gasoline evaporation after a spill depends on interacting environmental and geometric conditions that simplified empirical models may not represent adequately. This study develops PG-GERP, a prediction approach that combines physical modeling with machine learning. The framework uses physically meaningful information to describe how conditions such as wind, temperature, and spill dimensions affect evaporation. It supports evaporation-rate estimation for accidental releases and provides a basis for assessing the associated hazards.
