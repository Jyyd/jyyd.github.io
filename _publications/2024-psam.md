The HR-PM-TS framework combines monitoring and gridded data through stacked machine learning to estimate hourly PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations on a 1 km grid. The study evaluates population-weighted exposure, differences among age groups, and relationships with land cover. Stacking reduces prediction time relative to the deep-learning models examined. Population weighting increases estimated concentrations in many areas, while associations with built-up land and cropland highlight the relevance of human activity to exposure. The framework supports geographically detailed air-quality and health-risk assessment.
